Behind the Sanctuary

SafeBite AI is a project created by Sion and Daniel, Grade 9 students at Forest City International School (FCIS).

What began as an assignment assigned by our teacher has evolved into a passion project for animal welfare. We built this tool to simplify the complex world of pet safety, ensuring that owners everywhere have instant access to nature's wisdom and scientific safety data.
